To create a PodReaper instance that cleans up stuck Terminating pods
across all namespaces after 5 minutes:

  kubectl apply -f - <<EOF
  apiVersion: reaper.estadea.io/v1alpha1
  kind: PodReaper
  metadata:
    name: cluster-reaper
  spec:
    podStates:
      - Terminating
    graceTimeout: "5m"
    checkInterval: "30s"
  EOF

List all PodReaper instances:
  kubectl get podreaper   (or: kubectl get pr)
